# Adopters

Who is using AOBench, and for what. **Opt-in only** — nobody appears here without
asking to.

| Organisation | Use | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| University of Bologna (DEI) | Benchmark development; agent evaluation research | [@MSKazemi](https://github.com/MSKazemi) |

*This list is short because the project is young and has not been announced anywhere.
If you are using AOBench, adding yourself here genuinely helps: it tells other HPC
centres that someone else went first, and it tells us which parts of the benchmark are
load-bearing for real users.*

## Add yourself

Open a PR editing this file, or say so in
[Discussions](https://github.com/MSKazemi/aobench/discussions) and we will add you.
One line is enough:

```markdown
| Your organisation | What you use it for | @your-handle or a URL |
```

**You do not need permission, and you do not need to be doing anything impressive.**
"Evaluating whether an agent could help our helpdesk triage" is exactly the kind of
entry worth having.

If your use is under NDA or otherwise not public, an anonymous entry
("A European national HPC centre — vendor agent evaluation") is fine too. Tell us
privately and we will phrase it however you need.

## Using AOBench in a paper?

Please also:

- Cite the version you ran — [how to cite](../about/citation.md)
- Tell us in Discussions, so we can link your paper from the docs and from
  [related work](../about/related-work.md)

Papers that use AOBench and find it wanting are the most useful of all. We will link
those too.
